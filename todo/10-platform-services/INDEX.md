# 10 Platform Services

This domain holds **shared platform services** that sit above kernel primitives but below end-user applications: Win32 PE execution and API surface, shell DLL export planning, distribution (ISO, installer, updates), recovery and accessibility, Linux ELF compat, and long-range platform features (debugger, telemetry, and similar).

## Belongs Here

- Win32 subsystem bring-up (`TODO-07`, `TODO-08`) and DLL export master tables (`TODO-A` through `TODO-C`).
- Installer, updates, restore points, OOBE, and related control-panel style platform UX.
- Linux compatibility layer, accessibility, media subsystems, and stretch platform capabilities when they are cross-cutting (not a single app).

## Does Not Belong Here

- One-off app features or UX work. Put that in [11 Apps](../11-apps/INDEX.md).
