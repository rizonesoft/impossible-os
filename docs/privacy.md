# Privacy

This page says what impossibleos.co collects when you visit it, and who collects it. The site itself has no accounts, no forms and no advertising. It measures visits with Google Analytics, and everything else below comes from the services it is hosted on or loads.

## Who runs this site?

Impossible OS is published by Rizonetech (Pty) Ltd. Questions about this page go to the [project's issue tracker](https://github.com/rizonesoft/impossible-os/issues).

## Which services see my visit?

- **GitHub Pages** hosts the site and receives every request, including your IP address, as its server logs. See the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics** measure which pages are visited, from where and on what kind of device. Analytics records each page's full address, so a docs search you open as a full results page (`search.html?q=`) sends your search words to Google too. The frozen docs of past releases load no analytics. See [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **Google Fonts** serves the typefaces, so your browser sends Google a request for them. See the [Google Fonts privacy FAQ](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).
- **jsDelivr** serves the diagram library to documentation pages that contain a diagram.
- **hits.sh** draws the visitor counter at the foot of the landing page and counts the request.
- **PayPal** receives you only if you choose the Donate link.

## Does Google Analytics set cookies?

Everywhere else, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (`_ga` and `_ga_<measurement ID>`) to tell a returning visit from a new one. In the EEA (including Réunion, Guadeloupe, Martinique, French Guiana, Mayotte, Saint-Martin and the Åland Islands), the UK, Gibraltar, Guernsey, Jersey, the Isle of Man and Switzerland, the site asks Google for no analytics cookies at all: Google Analytics then sends cookieless measurements, which record that a page was viewed but cannot recognise you on a later visit. Nowhere does the site allow advertising storage or ad personalisation. You can block Google Analytics everywhere with [Google's opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) or any content blocker.

## Does the site store anything in my browser?

Apart from the analytics cookies above, the documentation pages remember your light or dark theme choice in your browser's local storage. It is never sent anywhere.
